# Welcome to My GitHub! 👋

I'm a passionate **data analyst** with a diverse background spanning **data analysis**, **data research**, **data engineering**, and **machine learning**. My journey in the world of data has equipped me with a unique blend of expertise across various domains.

## 📊 Data Analysis & Research

My journey into data began with **Microsoft Excel** and **PowerPoint**, where I developed a foundation in **data organization, analysis, and presentation**. As my interest in data grew, I transitioned to more robust analytical tools, including **SQL** for querying and managing relational databases and **Python** for data manipulation, statistical analysis, and automation. This transition enabled me to work with larger and more complex datasets, uncover meaningful patterns, and develop a more systematic, data-driven approach to research and problem-solving.

## 🤖 Machine Learning & Predictive Analytics

Building on my data analysis experience, I expanded my expertise into **machine learning** and predictive analytics. Using **Pandas** and **NumPy**, I perform data cleaning, preprocessing, transformation, and exploratory data analysis to prepare datasets for modeling. With **Scikit-learn**, **XGBoost**, and **CatBoost**, I develop and evaluate machine learning models, explore predictive patterns, and extract insights that support data-driven decision-making.

## 📈 Data Visualization & Business Intelligence

I believe that effective data analysis goes beyond extracting insights — it also requires communicating findings clearly and effectively. I use **Plotly** to create interactive visualizations that help explore datasets and reveal trends, patterns, and relationships. With **Power BI** and **Tableau**, I develop informative dashboards and reports that transform analytical results into accessible visual narratives, supporting data exploration, performance monitoring, and informed decision-making.

## ⚙️ Data Engineering & Workflow Automation

To support reproducible analysis and reliable data workflows, I work with tools such as **Git** and **GitHub** for version control and collaborative development, **Docker** for containerization and consistent execution environments, and **Apache Airflow** for orchestrating and automating data pipelines. These technologies help me organize analytical projects, manage dependencies, streamline repetitive tasks, and build maintainable workflows that support the entire data lifecycle — from data acquisition and processing to analysis and reporting.

🤝 Feel free to explore my repositories, discover my data-driven projects, and see how I turn raw data into meaningful insights. I'm always open to exchanging ideas, exploring new technologies, and collaborating on interesting projects in **data analytics, machine learning, and data engineering**!
